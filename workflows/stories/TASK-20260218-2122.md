## Task Summary

**What**: Add a disabled placeholder `Menu.Item` ("No models downloaded") when `modelStore.availableModels` is empty in the Benchmark screen's model selector, preventing a PaperMenu hang/freeze caused by rendering zero children.

**Why**: The react-native-paper `Menu` component enters an infinite layout/measurement loop when opened with zero children, causing the app to freeze.

**Where**:
- `src/screens/BenchmarkScreen/BenchmarkScreen.tsx`
- `src/screens/BenchmarkScreen/__tests__/BenchmarkScreen.test.tsx`

## Notes

- The root cause is that react-native-paper's `Menu` component cannot handle being opened with zero children -- it enters an infinite layout/measurement loop.
- The custom `Menu` wrapper at `src/components/Menu/Menu.tsx` passes children through to PaperMenu, so it inherits this behavior.
- The fix is minimal: a ternary that renders a single disabled `Menu.Item` when the list is empty. This gives PaperMenu a measurable child and prevents the infinite loop.
- The l10n key is added only to `en.json` -- other languages are managed by translators on Weblate and will fall back to English until translated.
- The mock `modelStore.availableModels` is a computed that filters `this.models` by `isDownloaded` (`__mocks__/stores/modelStore.ts:175-177`). Setting `models = []` is the simplest way to produce an empty result for the test.
