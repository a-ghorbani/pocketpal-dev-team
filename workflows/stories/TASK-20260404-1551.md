### Background
GitHub issue #661 requests upgrading llama.rn from 0.12.0-rc.2 to 0.12.0-rc.3 to add Gemma 4 model support. This brings the underlying llama.cpp from b8547 to b8641. GGUF quants are already available from Unsloth on HuggingFace (E2B ~3.5-4.7GB, E4B ~5GB at Q4_K_M). Audio is NOT supported in llama.cpp yet; text and vision are.
